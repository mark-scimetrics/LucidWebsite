Common questions

Can Lucid change anything in my Xero?

No. The connection is read-only. Lucid can’t create, edit or delete records, and it can’t move money.

Will a lender see my data?

Only if you choose to apply, and only the lender you approve. You see exactly what will be shared before anything is sent.

How do I disconnect?

From your Lucid settings or from Xero’s connected apps page, at any time. You can also ask us to delete your data.
