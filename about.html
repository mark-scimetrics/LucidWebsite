About us

Built by people who’ve sat on the lender’s side of the table.

Why we built Lucid

We’ve spent our careers inside banks and lenders: building the credit-risk models they use to decide who gets funded, and the collections and insolvency strategies they use when things go wrong.

The pattern was always the same. The problem was visible weeks earlier, in the business’s own numbers, and nobody was looking. By the time the owner noticed, the cheap options had gone, payments were being missed, and any application was made in a hurry, from weakness.

Accounting software is very good at recording what has happened. Very few small businesses have a finance director to tell them what’s coming. So we built Lucid to do three things: show your cash 13 weeks ahead, spot the issue early, and give you time to deal with it. Free fixes first. Funding only when it’s genuinely the answer, and then applied for calmly, in good order.

We can’t promise any business will be funded. We can give every business the best chance.
