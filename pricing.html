Questions

Straight answers on cost

Is the free plan really free?

Yes. The 13-week forecast and the weekly verdict are free for as long as you use them. We don’t ask for a card.

Does Lucid earn money from lenders?

Sometimes. If you choose to apply for funding through Lucid and the lender funds you, the lender may pay us a fee. We tell you before you apply, and we earn the same whichever lender you choose.

Can I share Lucid with my accountant?

Yes. On Lucid Plus you can invite your accountant to see your forecast and verdict, so their advice starts from the same numbers.

Can I cancel?

Yes, at any time. You keep the free forecast, and you can disconnect Xero and ask us to delete your data whenever you like.
