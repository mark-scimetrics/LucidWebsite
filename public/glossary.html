Glossary

Cashflow, in plain English.

The terms you’ll meet in Lucid and in our guides, explained in a sentence or two.

Cashflow forecast
An estimate of the money coming into and going out of your bank account over a future period, showing the balance you’ll have at each point.
13-week cashflow forecast
A week-by-week forecast covering the next quarter. Long enough to see VAT, payroll and quarterly bills coming; short enough to be accurate.
Cash runway
How long your current cash would last at your current rate of spending, if nothing else came in.
Lowest point
The smallest balance your forecast shows over the period. The single most useful number in any cashflow forecast.
Cash buffer
The minimum balance you choose to keep for safety. Many owners aim for at least one month of fixed costs.
Direct method
Building a forecast from the actual receipts and payments you expect, rather than adjusting profit figures. Best for short-term forecasts.
Aged debtors
A list of unpaid customer invoices grouped by how overdue they are, for example 0–30, 31–60 and 61–90 days.
Debtor days
The average number of days customers take to pay you. Lucid works this out for each customer separately.
Working capital
Your current assets (cash, money owed to you, stock) minus your current liabilities (bills, tax and short-term debt due within a year).
PAYE
Income tax and National Insurance deducted from wages. Usually due to HMRC by the 22nd of the following tax month if you pay electronically.
VAT quarter
The three-month period a VAT return covers. Payment is normally due one calendar month and seven days after the period ends.
Time to Pay
An arrangement with HMRC to pay a tax bill in instalments. Ask before the payment is due.
Invoice finance
Borrowing against unpaid customer invoices, so you get most of the money before the customer pays.
Statutory interest on late payment
Interest a business can claim on late business-to-business invoices under UK late payment law, currently 8% above the Bank of England base rate.
Payroll Safety Light
Lucid’s at-a-glance signal showing whether your next payroll, and the PAYE and pension that follow it, are covered.
Verdict
Lucid’s plain-English daily answer: nothing to do, or act this week, with the reason and the amount.
