How it works

From Xero to your next 13 weeks in minutes.

No spreadsheets, no assumptions to maintain, no finance degree needed. Lucid does the forecasting and tells you what it means.

Build your forecast freeSee a 60-second demo
Step 1 · Connect

Connect Xero with read-only access

Two clicks through Xero’s own sign-in. Lucid reads your invoices, bills, bank transactions and payroll. It can’t change your books or move money, and you can disconnect at any time.

Step 2 · Five questions

Tell Lucid what your books can’t

Five plain multiple-choice questions cover the things a ledger doesn’t know: a big contract about to start, a seasonal dip, a planned hire. Change any answer later just by typing it in chat.

Step 3 · Forecast

A 13-week forecast you can check

Lucid’s AI writes the forecast as code, and the code calculates every number from your data. It learns when each customer really pays, not just when invoices fall due, and places VAT, PAYE and Corporation Tax on their real dates. Every figure can be traced back to where it came from.

Step 4 · Daily verdict

One answer every morning

“Nothing to do, you’re fine” on most days. “Act this week” when something material changes, with the reason and the amount. The Payroll Safety Light shows at a glance whether the next payroll is covered.

Step 5 · Ways through

The cheapest fix comes first

  • Move a payment: re-time a supplier run or agree a date with HMRC.
  • Chase invoices: who owes what, and who to call first.
  • Fund it: only if needed, an impartial panel of lenders and a lender-ready pack, sent only with your permission.
You stay in control

Your business, your assumptions.

Lucid advises; you decide. Ask “what if we win the Henderson contract?” or “what if we hire in March?” and see the 13 weeks change in seconds.

What does Lucid read from Xero?

Invoices, bills, bank transactions, contacts, payroll journals and your chart of accounts, with read-only access. It cannot create, edit or delete anything in Xero.

What are the five questions?

Short multiple-choice questions about things your books can’t tell us, such as whether you expect a big one-off payment, how seasonal your sales are, or whether you’re about to hire. You can change any answer later by chat.

How accurate is the forecast?

Every week Lucid compares its forecast with what actually cleared your bank and adjusts. Accuracy improves the longer you’re connected. You can always see the assumptions behind any number.

How long does it take?

Most businesses see their first forecast within a few minutes of connecting. Larger ledgers can take a little longer the first time.
